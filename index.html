Dennison Bertram

About

Dennison Bertram is a founder, developer evangelist, and builder. He likes to work at the edge of new technologies — where the protocols haven't settled, the tools don't exist yet, and the obvious answer is to build the thing yourself.

He's spent the last decade founding and scaling developer-facing products at frontier moments: an early Bitcoin exchange in 2012, the first Developer Advocate at OpenZeppelin, the low-code Ethereum tooling at DappHero, and most recently as founder and CEO of Tally.xyz — the DAO platform that became the standard for on-chain governance, transferring over $1b, and helping teams govern over $40b.

Before software, Dennison spent years as an internationally published fashion photographer and creative technologist, working with Elle, Vogue, Gucci, Louis Vuitton, Heineken, GQ, and others. He still cares deeply about how things look and feel.

He lives in Brooklyn and is reachable at dennison@dennisonbertram.com.
